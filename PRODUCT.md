# SA21 product context

## Register

product

## Users

Roof-ER sales reps use this installed phone app at doors and on roofs, often one-handed,
in bright daylight and unreliable reception. Managers and marketing staff use its
role-scoped administration tools. Homeowners receive separate inspection and profile pages.

## Product Purpose

Help reps find field information, communicate with homeowners, document inspections,
and follow through. Success means completing the task with accurate information and
clear confirmation, without losing work or searching through unrelated tools.

## Brand Personality

Direct, capable, grounded. Preserve the field-manager voice and actual Roof-ER assets.

## Anti-references

Decorative AI command centers, glowing glass cards, invented metrics, generic motivational
card grids, and controls that promise actions they cannot perform.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful action before supporting information.
2. Make frequent destinations reachable by thumb and keyboard.
3. Distinguish unavailable, loading, failed, and successful states.
4. Keep work recoverable and permissions unchanged.
5. Migrate one surface at a time; preserve the established identity while theme choices remain open.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, including readable contrast, visible focus, meaningful labels,
44px touch targets, keyboard-complete dialogs, status announcements, and reduced motion.

Grounded in the SA21 pickup, existing role/feature rules, and the settled Roof-ER design research.
